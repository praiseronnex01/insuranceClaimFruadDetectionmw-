Case Overview

Investigation Objective and Scope

Case Scenario

A sample disk image was provided for an educational digital-forensics investigation. The scenario concerns a suspected unauthorised file transfer from a workstation to external storage.

The investigator preserved the evidence, examined relevant artifacts, reconstructed activity, and prepared a forensic report.

Investigation Scope

• Verify disk-image integrity
• Maintain chain of custody
• Identify files and metadata
• Recover deleted artifacts
• Review logs and user activity
• Create an event timeline
• Document findings and limitations

Legal and ethical notice: This investigation uses only a legally obtained forensic training dataset or instructor-authorised sample image. No live systems, private personal data, or unauthorised evidence were used.
